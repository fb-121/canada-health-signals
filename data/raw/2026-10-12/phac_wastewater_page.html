About these data tiles

The activity levels of COVID-19, flu and RSV at the collection site level are determined using an activity level index. We use a population-weighted average to calculate activity levels at the city, province, territory and national level. For more details, please refer to the technical notes.

Interpreting wastewater data

The wastewater dashboard shows the concentration of COVID-19, flu and RSV in wastewater samples from different sites. The solid line of the graphs shows the population-weighted EpiWeek average of the viral load for Canada and for each city and province or territory. We calculate this by averaging the viral loads within a week and adjusting for population served by each site.

The grey shaded area of each graph shows the minimum and maximum COVID-19, flu and RSV signals in wastewater of each EpiWeek since the beginning of surveillance based on available data.

  • Use the hover option of each graph to see the daily value, as well as historical minimum and maximum values. Due to the potential for changes in viral shedding over time, caution should be used when comparing wastewater monitoring data to historic trends in clinical cases.
  • Any breaks in trend lines of graphs are due to limited data for that particular time period. Use caution when interpreting short-term changes in viral load. An ongoing increase or decrease in the viral load is more reliable for indicating trends.

Wastewater data should be considered in combination with clinical sources for respiratory viral data.

Virus gene copies in wastewater represents how much a particular virus is present within the community captured by a sewer system. It can provide insights into the presence and extent to which a virus is being shed into a sewer system but it cannot tell us how many people are sick and/or hospitalized.

For detailed epidemiological data (such as clinical testing data, severity indicators, hospitalizations) and trends on influenza, COVID-19, and RSV in Canada, refer to Canadian respiratory virus surveillance report.
