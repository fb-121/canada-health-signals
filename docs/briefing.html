Facts versus analysis. Everything outside the shaded analysis boxes comes from an official record. Analysis boxes are interpretation, written by a reviewer, labelled with who wrote them and whether they were reviewed.

FDA labels. FDA-listed: on FDA's AI-enabled device list. Document-verified: the FDA 510(k) summary describes AI or machine learning (the summary may name a method such as computer vision). Manufacturer-reported: the device or company name claims AI, unchecked. Inferred: a guess from an AI-heavy product code. AI unknown: no evidence either way.

Financing labels. Derived from the Form D's own security-type flags and an EDGAR lookup. A listed company's private placement is not a startup round. An amendment updates an existing offering and is not a new raise. Late means more than 15 days after the first sale, adjusted for weekends only. Startups that file under Other Technology are missed.
